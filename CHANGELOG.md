# Changelog

Each version is released on GitHub with a Windows installer and a zip. The download page shows the newest section of this file, so keep the newest version at the top.

## 2.5.0 · Showtime · 2026-10-15

**Every big moment now hits like one.**

- **Entering an arena**: two hazard doors with the round and arena name on a lit plate, then they slam open in a burst of sparks.
- **Countdown**: 3, 2, 1 stamp onto the screen, then **FIGHT!** explodes with light rays, a shockwave and a flash.
- **Round won, round lost, wrecked, boss fight, wave cleared**: titles slam in letter by letter with colour ghosts, a shine, cinema bars, light rays, a shockwave and sparks. A win rains confetti; a loss glows red with embers.
- **Victory and Defeat** at the end of a match get the full show, with a confetti storm for a win.
- **Open world**: entering the world, mission complete, mission failed, bosses, raider camps, epic and legendary finds, nightfall and morning all get the same treatment.
- **Best graphics by default**: full resolution, all effects and animations on. If the game runs slowly it now only suggests Performance mode instead of switching it on by itself, and older saves move back up to full quality once.
- The garage outline behind the chassis is steel grey instead of black.
- The main menu fits on shorter screens (720 to 840 pixels high): the open world button no longer hides under the radio ticker.

## 2.4.0 · Night Shift · 2026-10-15

**Polish for the whole game, and the open world comes alive.**

- **Day and night** in the open world: dusk turns the sky orange, at night your headlights, camps and raider fires light the way. More raiders come out at night, and the crates hold better parts.
- **Loot with a kick**: crates and safes burst into splinters, parts hop out and drift toward you, rare parts send up a beam of light you can spot from far away. What you pick up flies into your My parts counter.
- **Travel between camps**: every camp you have found is on the camp menu; pick one and you are there (not with raiders on your tail or a crate on board).
- **Missions feel like missions**: a progress bar and checkpoint pips on the HUD, a burst at every checkpoint, and confetti, a coin fountain and a cheer when you finish. Epic and legendary finds get their own moment.
- **Camps that look lived-in**: a turning ring, a flag in the wind, a beacon and welding sparks. Raider camps have a fire you see from afar.
- **Raiders talk**: they shout when they spot you and complain when you wreck them.
- **Tyre smoke and skid marks** when you slide on hard floors, in every arena.
- **Smoother driving in the open world**: new ground is painted three times faster, so entering new areas no longer stutters.
- A tidier HUD in the open world: rounded panels, a sun and moon clock, a health bar that shows what you just lost, a scrap counter that counts up.
- The open world is no longer marked as a test; the menu shows your missions and parts.
- **Website**: a new open world section with screenshots, and the page now also opens straight from a folder.

## 2.3.0 · Scrap Roads · 2026-10-15

**The open world, now with something to do (and someone to do it with).**

- **Buildings to loot**: walled houses, sheds and shops with doors. Inside are loot crates and the odd heavy safe. Break them open: the parts you drive over go straight into **My parts**. Some buildings are guarded.
- **Field garage**: at every camp, press *Enter* (or the camp button) to open the camp. *Garage* takes you into the builder with the parts you found; *Back to the world* drops you back where you were.
- **Random encounters**: raider camps in the wild, slow armoured **convoys** with good loot, and **bounty hunters** who come for you after a few wrecks.
- **Missions** on every camp's board: delivery runs, clearing a raider camp, road races, and every fourth job a **boss**. Rewards are scrap and rare parts.
- **Play together**: in the online lobby pick *Open world*, choose your car and press *Start together*. You share the same map, crates, missions and rewards; every part one of you finds gives the other a part of the same rarity.
- **Your world is saved**: leave whenever you like and pick *Continue* in the menu. Map, parts, car and missions come back.

## 2.2.0 · Off the Map (test) · 2026-10-15

**Open world (test mode)**: a new tile in the main menu.

- One big map (20×20 km of arena, so to speak) stitched together from every arena theme, generated fresh each time from a seed.
- **Biomes**: snow and ice in the north, desert, lava and beaches in the south, towns, yards and gardens in between, and now and then a chessboard, toy room or haunted house. Each biome has its own floor (and so its own grip and speed for your tyres), props, ramps, gimmicks and music.
- **Roads** cross the map; asphalt is the fast way around. **Camps** sit on every crossing: drive onto one to repair fully and make it your respawn point.
- **Raiders** roam the land and come after you when you get close or shoot them. Further from the start they get tougher. Wrecking one pays scrap, sometimes a repair kit.
- **Minimap** with fog: it fills in as you explore. Big place names when you enter a biome.
- Pick your car: the one in your garage, a saved blueprint or a ready-made one.
- The world streams in around you in 1×1 km chunks, so only the area near you is simulated; what you wreck stays wrecked while you are nearby.

**Online rooms are simpler**

- Four one-click setups: Duel, Quick brawl, Team up, Team vs team. Mode, length and teams fold away under "Change details".
- Your friend sees exactly what you picked, a Copy code button, and clearer buttons (Leave room, Waiting for your friend…).

**Also**

- The main menu shows the modes in a 3×2 grid.

## 2.1.4 · Turn It Around · 2026-10-14

- **Parts visibly turn**: a part you turn on the car swings round instead of snapping.
- **Turn next** now turns the picture in the inspector too, and tells you which way the next part will face (and how to turn one that is already on the car).
- **"Parts bin" is now "My parts"**, everywhere.
- **Back to the garage** (after a crate, or from the waiting arena) always lands on My parts.

## 2.1.3 · Smooth Garage · 2026-10-14

- **Fixed: the top bar was gone in the garage and in matches** after visiting the main menu, taking the coins, the round, Codex, Pause, Menu and the fullscreen button with it.
- **Fullscreen**: the game now starts fullscreen. There is a Fullscreen/Window switch in the main menu, the ⛶ button in the top bar is back, and F11 still works.
- **Animations always on unless you turn them off**: Windows' "animation effects" switch (often off on laptops) used to strip almost every transition from the game. Now only Settings → Interface animations decides.
- **New transitions**: the garage assembles itself when you enter it (panels slide in, the bay rises, your parts are dealt), the parts list re-deals when you switch category or tab, and the inspector slides each new part in.
- Fixed: wheels, spikes and other open parts no longer sit on black squares in the garage either.

## 2.1.2 · No More Black Boxes · 2026-10-14

- Fixed: wheels, spikes, rams and other parts that do not fill their cell sat on a black square in battle. The dark outline and the shadow now follow only the solid parts of the chassis.

## 2.1.1 · Parts That Move · 2026-10-14

- **Every weapon now moves when it fires.** Gun barrels kick back with a muzzle flash, the minigun barrels spin as they spool up, and rocket pods, mortars, launchers and the odd weapons (tuba, piano, toaster…) pop with a puff of smoke. Flamethrowers, water cannons and beams shudder while they fire.
- **Abilities react too**: smoke, flare, decoy and EMP launchers, the air horn, the blink drive and the droppers (mines, banana peels, oil) pop when used.
- **Wheels and tracks roll**: the tread turns with your speed and direction.
- A new test checks that all 62 weapons visibly react when they fire.

## 2.1.0 · Know Your Ground · 2026-10-14

**Every arena has its own floor**

- **Sand, mud, snow, ice, grass, dirt, tiles, steel, wood, carpet, rock and asphalt.** Each floor changes your speed and grip.
- **Tyres matter.** Off-road tyres and tracks float over sand and mud, spiked tyres bite on ice and snow, and slicks are fastest on asphalt but sink in sand. Hover pads ignore the floor.
- **The garage window shows the next floor** and how your current tyres do on it. The VS screen names the best tyres for the arena.
- **The beach has three zones**: firm wet sand by the water, soft sand in the middle and a fast wooden boardwalk.
- **Bots pick tyres that suit the arena.**
- Loose floors kick up sand, snow or mud behind your wheels, and leave tracks in the matching colour.

**Arenas look the part**

- **New arena: Grand Chessboard.** A giant board with white and black pieces. The kings do not move for anyone. It replaces the second, duplicate ice rink.
- **Boardwalk Beach**: towels, parasols, sandcastles, lifebuoys, surfboards, coolers, palm trees, shells and footprints.
- **Ice Rink**: hockey goals at both ends, pucks to slap around, corner bumpers and a penguin.
- New props elsewhere: car wrecks in the junkyards and the car park, rotten logs in the swamp, cauldrons in the haunted house, cow skulls in the desert, AC units on the roof, giant mugs in the kitchen and penguins on the snow.

## 2.0.0 · The Big One · 2026-10-14

**New ways to play**

- **League**: a season of five matchdays against the same five rivals, with a table, rival matches played off-screen, a final for the top two, prizes, a title and the Dynasty achievement for three seasons.
- **Daily challenge**: one seed a day, so everyone gets the same arena, rival and drafts, plus one odd rule (Glass Cannons, Bumper Cars, Tank Battle…). Your best score and your streak are kept, and the first play of the day pays out.
- **Gauntlet co-op**: run the Gauntlet with a bot partner. Every wave brings two rivals.
- **Local duel**: player 1 on keyboard and mouse, player 2 on a controller, on one PC. The Online tile is now Friends.
- **Workshop contracts**: three small jobs a day with bolts as pay, and a Mk II chip for finishing all three.

**More to fight**

- **Twelve Gauntlet bosses, each with its own trick**: Mega Mauler charges, Grandma Tank raises a shield, the Shredder's magnet drags you in, Professor Overkill calls in artillery, Inferno Ingrid leaves burning puddles, King Kongcrete slams the ground, plus six new ones: Ghost Rider Gus (invisible), The Tower (repairs itself), Mirror Max (drives a copy of your car), Minefield Mona, Hurricane Harriet (throws couches) and The Press (hits harder as it breaks). Every attack is telegraphed.
- **Six new arenas**: Crusher Yard, Frozen Parking Lot, Neon Speedway, Warp Lab, Funfair Bumper Cars and Glacier Pass, with three new arena tricks: portals, black ice and boost rings.
- **Rivals remember you**: every bot keeps your head-to-head record and radios in on the VS screen with a line that fits. Your profile has a rivals album.

**Sound and feel**

- **Music for every arena**: nine generated styles (metal, ice, lava, neon, toy, spooky, desert, surf and a calm garage track) that build up in the last 30 seconds, at low health and in the final round.
- **Screens fade out** when they close, instead of blinking away.
- **Photo mode** (F2 or the pause menu): freeze, pan, zoom, six filters, save a PNG.

**Desktop**

- **Controller support**: drive, aim and use every part in battle, and move through all menus with the d-pad or stick.
- **Set your own keys** in Settings → Keys and controller.
- **A real save file** in your profile folder, with three rotating back-ups, so progress survives clearing data or reinstalling. Settings can open the save folder.
- **Error log**: problems are written to errors.log; Settings → Report a problem opens it.

**Also**

- Fourteen new achievements and four new titles.
- Fixed: the menu fight no longer uses dark arenas, which looked like an empty screen.

## 1.5.0 · Live from the Pit · 2026-10-12

- **A living main menu**: two bots fight live in the pit behind the menu, with a live scoreboard in the main card. Your car turns on a pedestal under a swaying work light while a welder throws sparks, and the PIT FM board carries the casters' banter and tips. Performance mode turns the glass blur off.
- **Pick A or B at level 5**: every part now has two real play styles, like Hot Rounds or Tracer Rounds on the Machine Gun. Switching later costs 40 bolts. Old "Steady" picks became the B option.
- **Mod chips**: gear opens a chip socket at level 3, 6 and 9. Chips (Power, Plating, Turbo, Lucky, Ember, Static, Mender, Crowd) come in Mk I, II and III; three of a kind fuse into the next mark in the new Chips tab. You find them after matches, and every Gauntlet boss drops a Mk II.
- **Loadout sets**: three guns turn on Lead Storm, two explosives Demolition Crew, three armour pieces Bunker, and more. The Garage shows which sets are on and which ones you are close to.
- **Duplicates are never wasted**: a part you already own becomes XP for every copy of it.
- **Reforge**: a level 10 part can go back to level 1 with a permanent star (+2% each, up to five stars).

## 1.4.0 · Tag Team · 2026-10-11

- **2v2**: fight two bots with a bot partner (pick its style: Gunner, Rammer, Artillery or Brawler), or play a friend online in 2v2. In the lobby each player picks Blue or Red: same side means you and your friend against two bots, different sides means each of you gets a bot partner. No friendly fire; a team loses when both cars are wrecked.
- **Gear 2.0**: parts now go to level 10, each level with its own colour. Parts earn XP by riding along in your matches; wins pay the bolts. With a full XP bar you upgrade. Weapons get +3% damage per level, armour +5% HP, movement +1% speed. Every part has a special at level 5 (or the safe "Steady" pick) and a second one at level 10, like double bullets on the Machine Gun. Bolts and Scrap Cred sit side by side at the top.
- **New workbench**: your part on a stage inside a ring of its ten level colours, the level in big numbers, and one clear upgrade button that says what you still need. Less table data.
- **Better looking parts**: blocks and modules got a rim light, bolts, shadows and seams; cars get an outline and a drop shadow.
- **Fewer modes, all sharper**: Duel (1v1 or 2v2), Blueprint Brawl, Scrap Gauntlet and Online. Story mode (Scrap Road) and the sandbox are gone. Their stickers now unlock by player level, and there are new achievements: win a 2v2, reach wave 15, beat a Hard bot and beat King Kongcrete.
- After a match the game offers an easier or harder bot when you keep losing or winning.
- Fixed: an arena hazard could now and then spawn inside a wall.

## 1.3.0 · Showtime · 2026-10-11

- **Real commentator voices**: Dash Danvers and Doc Gasket are now recorded neural voices instead of the robotic system voice, with a booth sound, and the music ducks while they talk. The arena announcer got a deep stadium voice with echo.
- **112 new commentary lines**, funnier and more random: "Two engineering disasters enter. One insurance claim leaves!", "THAT PART HAS LEFT THE CONVERSATION!", "Held together by one bolt and incredible confidence!". New moments: clutch survival on almost no health, the last ten seconds, and random banter and sponsor reads when it goes quiet.
- **New caption**: the commentator's badge, a live light and a level meter; the words appear as they are spoken, and Dash's shouts shake. Fixed the text sometimes sitting outside its box.
- **Move the whole car** in the garage: arrow keys or the new pad move every part at once, the middle button centres the car, and you can turn it a quarter turn or swap left and right. Moves that would leave the bay bounce back.
- **A livelier interface**: screens slide in, menu tiles deal in, buttons react when you press them, yellow buttons glint, the main button breathes, coins and bolts pop when they change. Settings → Decorative motion turns it all off.

## 1.2.0 · Garage HQ · 2026-10-10

- **New main menu**: your car in a hangar, one Continue button for the Scrap Road, every mode as a tile with its options right below.
- **Garage HQ** replaces the Workbench and the Locker. Pick a **gear loadout** of 3 items (4 from level 10) that is in your parts bin from round 1. Lock gear you want to keep, build new gear in the **Fabricator**, and save up to three looks.
- **Blueprint Brawl**: bring one car worth up to 700 coins. No drafting and no shop. Against a bot or online.
- **Online**: quick chat (T, then 1–4), a rematch button and a head-to-head record per friend.
- **Scrap Road** is an illustrated map: click a mission to see it, then start it.
- **18 new parts**, including the Nail Storm, Seeker Darts, Pile Fist and Chrome Shield Plate. **3 new arenas**: Ice Rink, Scrap Press Plant and Stormy Rooftop. 4 new stickers.
- **Simpler controls**: logic chips and wires are gone. Only moving parts (hinges, rotators, rams) take a key. All weapons fire with left click.
- **Smarter building**: bots put their best armour at the front. "Add bin parts to my car" pairs wheels, points melee forward and leaves moving parts to you.
- Removed: the Lives, Bounty and Hill formats, and the drivable map.

## 1.1.0 · Scrap Road · 2026-10-09

- **Scrap Road** story mode: 19 missions in 4 regions, each region ending with a boss (Mother Crusher, the Twin Drill, the Magnet Baron, the Junk King). Missions have their own arenas, special rounds, salvage crates and three stars.
- **Gear**: parts you find are yours to keep. Upgrade them with bolts to a level 5 Masterwork, pick a quirk at level 3, fuse duplicates or scrap them.
- **Stickers and skins**: stickers show on your car and give small perks; two from your skin's set unlock a set bonus.
- **The show**: commentators Dash Danvers and Doc Gasket call the fight, the crowd cheers and boos, and a full hype meter gets you a gift.
- Battle telemetry: speed, damage per second out and in, parts left.

## 1.0.0 · Desktop app · 2026-10-09

- Scrap Duel becomes a Windows app. Everything runs on your PC. Solo, Scrap Gauntlet and Sandbox work offline.

- Saved window size, F11 for fullscreen, and an automatic performance mode for slow screens.
