Build a battle car from scrap. Then wreck someone else's.

Draft parts, bolt them onto a grid and drive what you made into a bot, a boss or a friend. Every round you get new scrap and a chance to build something better.

How a match plays

A duel runs 5 to 25 rounds. Every round has the same three parts, and your car grows with each one.

Four ways to play

Everything except Online works without an internet connection.

  • Duel Draft, build and fight a bot on Easy, Normal or Hard. Alone, or 2v2 with a bot partner.1v1 or 2v2
  • Blueprint Brawl No drafting. Bring one car worth up to 700 coins and see whose design holds up.Against a bot or online
  • Scrap Gauntlet Endless waves with three hearts. A boss every fifth wave drops a relic.Roguelike
  • Online Play a friend with a 4-letter room code: 1v1, or 2v2 on the same team or against each other, each with a bot partner.Peer to peer

Your garage keeps what you find

Parts you win in matches stay yours. Garage HQ is where you make them better.

  • Gear loadout: three favourite parts in your bin from the first round.
  • Upgrades to level 10: parts earn XP in your matches, wins pay the bolts. Specials at level 5 and 10.
  • Fabricator: turn spare bolts into new gear.
  • Looks: skins, stickers with small perks, trails and wreck effects.

Install in a minute

  1. Download

    Get ScrapDuel-Setup.exe with the button below. It is about 100 MB.

  2. Run it

    Windows may say it protected your PC, because the game isn't signed by a big publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Play

    Start Scrap Duel from the desktop shortcut. Your saves stay on your PC, also after updates.
