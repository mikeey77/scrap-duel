Build a battle car from scrap. Then wreck someone else's.

Draft parts, bolt them onto a grid and drive what you made into a bot, a boss or a friend. Or take it out into the open world and come back with a bootful of parts.

Download for Windows

For Windows 10 and 11. Prefer no installer? Get the zip.

Then drive off the map

The open world stitches every arena into one huge map. Loot buildings for parts, rebuild your car at camps, take jobs and wreck raiders, alone or with a friend. It saves where you left it.

  • LootBreak crates and safes in buildings. Parts hop out, rare ones send up a beam.
  • Field garageAt any camp, open the garage and rebuild with what you found.
  • JobsDeliveries, raider camps, road races and every fourth job a boss.
  • RaidersRoaming gangs, armoured convoys and bounty hunters who come looking for you.
  • Day and nightAt night more raiders are out and the crates hold better parts.
  • Co-opSame map, same jobs, shared rewards: every find also drops a part for your friend.

How a match plays

A duel runs 5 to 25 rounds. Every round has the same three parts, and your car grows with each one.

Seven ways to play

Everything except online matches works without an internet connection.

  • Duel Draft, build and fight a bot on Easy, Normal or Hard. Alone, or 2v2 with a bot partner.1v1 or 2v2
  • Blueprint Brawl No drafting. Bring one car worth up to 700 coins and see whose design holds up.Against a bot or online
  • Scrap Gauntlet Endless waves with three hearts and twelve bosses, each with its own trick. Alone or with a bot partner.Roguelike · co-op
  • League A season of five matchdays against the same five rivals. Top the table, win the final, earn a title.Season
  • Daily challenge One seed a day: same arena, rival and drafts for everyone, plus one odd rule.New every day
  • Open world One huge map to explore with loot, camps, jobs, raiders and bosses. Saves as you go.Alone or co-op
  • Friends Online with a 4-letter room code (1v1, 2v2 or the open world together), or a local duel on one PC with a controller.Online or couch

Your garage keeps what you find

Parts you win in matches stay yours. Garage HQ is where you make them better.

  • Gear loadout: three favourite parts in your bin from the first round.
  • Upgrades to level 10: parts earn XP in your matches, wins pay the bolts. Specials at level 5 and 10.
  • Fabricator: turn spare bolts into new gear.
  • Looks: skins, stickers with small perks, trails and wreck effects.

Install in a minute

    Windows may say it protected your PC, because the game isn't signed by a big publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway.
