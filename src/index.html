How to play

SCRAP DUEL

Weld a battle car together in your bay. Chassis parts go on the bottom layer; guns and gadgets bolt on top of support blocks. Draft a branch every round, win coins, spend them on parts, crates and a bigger bay.

  1. DraftFlip three cards, take one
  2. BuildNo clock. Shop with your coins
  3. ReadyTest-drive in the waiting arena
  4. FightTeleport into a random junkyard
Garage
Click to place, drag to paint, right-click removes. R turns the next part, T rotates placed ones. Scroll to zoom.
Drive
WASD or arrows drive. Mouse aims; left-click or Space fires. Equipped gadgets and their cooldowns appear in battle. J jumps; right-click activates gadgets or reels a harpoon.
Moving parts
Hinges, rotators, rams and springs move everything bolted beyond them. Bind any of them, or a weapon, to keys 1–9 with the Keys tool (G). Logic chips flip keys by themselves; connect them with the Wire tool (L).

Pick 5, 10 or 25 rounds. Each round is worth one point; the final round is worth two. Play a bot rival, a friend online, survive the Scrap Gauntlet, or mess around in the sandbox.
